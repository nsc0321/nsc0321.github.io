[Core Principle] MangaTranslator respects and protects your privacy. All manga images, translated outputs, custom glossaries, and optional user API keys are processed and stored strictly on your device. We NEVER upload, collect, or store your image files on any developer server.

1. Data Collection and Sharing (Google Play Data Safety)

MangaTranslator does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or user image files.

To support free development via banner and interstitial ads, the app uses Google AdMob SDK, which collects and shares the following data with Google:

Optional Quality Mode (User-Provided Gemini API Key):

  • Only when you explicitly enter your own Gemini API key and choose Quality Mode, extracted speech-bubble text is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API using your key.
  • Fast Mode (Manga-OCR + ML Kit) runs 100% offline on your device after model download.

2. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Used for Google AdMob ads, downloading on-device OCR/translation models, and optional user-initiated Gemini API calls.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network availability before ad or model requests.
  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): Used solely to read the image folder you select and export translated images to your chosen folder.

3. Third-Party Services (Google LLC)

This app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Data handling by Google is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

• Google Privacy Policy
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Data Retention and Deletion

No developer backend server is operated. All local models, glossaries, settings, and optional API keys remain on your device and are permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Your Rights and Opting Out of Personalized Ads

  • Android 12 or newer: Settings > Security & Privacy > Privacy > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID.
  • Android 11 or older: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Children's Privacy

MangaTranslator is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
